EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK EXCERPT. Fictional company policies.

Paid Time Off. Employees accrue 1.25 days of paid time off per month, up to a maximum balance of 20 days. Unused paid time off above the maximum balance does not roll over.

Non-Solicitation. For twelve (12) months after separation, the employee shall not solicit any customer with whom the employee had material contact during the prior two years of employment.

Remote Work. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
