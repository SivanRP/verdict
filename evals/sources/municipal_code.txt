RIVERBEND MUNICIPAL CODE, SECTION 14-203: SHORT-TERM RENTALS. Riverbend and this ordinance are fictional.

(a) No person may operate a short-term rental without a permit issued by the Department of Housing.

(b) A permit is valid for one (1) year and may be renewed annually.

(c) A short-term rental may not be occupied by more than two adults per bedroom, plus two additional adults.

(d) Hosts must respond to neighbor complaints within 60 minutes between 10 p.m. and 7 a.m.

(e) A first violation is punishable by a fine of up to $500. Each subsequent violation within twelve (12) months is punishable by a fine of up to $1,000.
